LOCAL-ONLY DEPENDENCY (not in this repository):
- "Ultimate Nature – Starter" v1.3.0 by Innerverse Interactive, Unity Asset Store https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/environments/landscapes/ultimate-nature-starter-176906 (free), Standard Unity Asset Store EULA. Raw files are NOT redistributed: Assets/InnerverseInteractive/ and the wrapper prefabs Assets/ValVert/Prefabs/uns_* are git-ignored. To rebuild: add the pack to your Unity account and import it (the package's Packages/manifest.json, Demo and Settings folders are not needed). Used for: spruce trees (UNS_Spruce_01/02) and bushes (UNS_Bush). Without it the build falls back to the CC0 Poly Haven fir_tree_01 / shrub_02.
