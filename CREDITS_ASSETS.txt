- Dam, tail fin, stone house, bollards, fences, bus shelter, and lamp posts: procedural Python meshes (self-made).
- Signs (Aldi, Lidl, Grand Frais, École Barral, Sibra bus, pharmacy cross), board-formed concrete, beige stone, green fence, striped bollard, ornamental grass, shop front, and school facade: self-made PIL textures, derived from the Poly Haven CC0 concrete_wall_008 and rock_wall_08 where applicable.
